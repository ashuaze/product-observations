### 1/4

1/4 YouTube’s “Not interested” button raises a product question. After I reject a topic on Home, should it also disappear from the suggestions beside a video I’m still watching? The answer changes what “this control works” means.

### 2/4

2/4 YouTube says Home recommendations rely primarily on watch history. Up Next relies mainly on the video currently playing. If you offer feedback across both, decide how a stated preference should interact with what someone is watching now.

### 3/4

3/4 A 2023 paper’s automated audit found repeated “Not interested” feedback cut target-topic recommendations on Home by 88% on average. It found no significant reduction beside a fixed video. This was a historical, controlled setup.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14551

### 4/4

4/4 Test the same feedback on Home and beside the player, holding the current video constant. Then change it. Measure unwanted repeats, useful related videos lost, and clicks needed to get relief. A cleaner Home feed can leave the next viewing session unresolved.
