### 1/3

1/3 A Notion export can preserve content while leaving a workflow to rebuild. Full-page databases export as CSV in Markdown & CSV mode. Notion says reuploading an export won't instantly recreate the workspace.

https://www.notion.com/help/export-your-content

### 2/3

2/3 A new CSV import can't create relations, rollups, or formulas. Importing into an existing database can map a column to an existing relation property. Importing rows and rebuilding the data model are different jobs.

https://www.notion.com/help/import-data-into-notion

### 3/3

3/3 A readable archive may be enough for a finished project. To keep working, test a small example after import and any rebuilding: can a task still link to its project, and does a calculated total still work? Export success should name the intended use.
