### 1/4

1/4 Netflix replaced five-star ratings with thumbs up/down in 2017. In 2022 it added Double Thumbs Up. The design question is how much preference people can express before the effort of answering costs you useful feedback.

### 2/4

2/4 Netflix reported a 200% increase in ratings activity when testing thumbs against stars. The test changed both the number of options and how ratings were framed, so it doesn’t isolate the effect of fewer choices.

https://about.netflix.com/en/news/goodbye-stars-hello-thumbs

### 3/4

3/4 Double Thumbs Up gives “Love this!” a separate answer from “I like this.” Netflix says it helps make recommendations more specific. Loving a film could mean its cast, tone, or ending. The extra answer captures intensity while leaving the reason open.

### 4/4

4/4 Before adding a stronger positive rating, test whether people distinguish it consistently and value the resulting recommendations. Measure rating participation too. Keep the extra choice if its gains outweigh any drop in responses.
