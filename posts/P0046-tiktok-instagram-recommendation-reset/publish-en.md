### 1/5

1/5 Your feed is stuck on an interest you've outgrown, but you still like the creators you follow. A recommendation reset has to decide what to keep. TikTok and Instagram's published designs make that choice visible.

### 2/5

2/5 TikTok's 2023 refresh announcement says it would restart For You recommendations while preserving enabled settings and followed accounts. A fresh feed can keep deliberate choices intact.

https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-a-way-to-refresh-your-for-you-feed-on-tiktok-us?lang=en

### 3/5

3/5 Instagram's 2024 reset announcement covers Explore, Reels and Feed. The flow also lets people review followed accounts and choose whom to unfollow. That makes editing explicit choices a separate decision.

https://about.fb.com/news/2024/11/introducing-recommendations-reset-instagram/

### 4/5

4/5 Both teams describe recommendations learning again from new interactions. A reset can change the starting point while keeping personalization going. My read: broad repair may save repeated corrections, but useful preferences may also need rebuilding.

### 5/5

5/5 For one unwanted topic in an otherwise useful feed, test a narrower correction first. For a broadly stale feed, test a reset. Measure unwanted repeats, useful discoveries and recovery effort over several sessions—not just novelty on the next screen.
