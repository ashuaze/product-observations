### 1/3

1/3 Send an unlisted YouTube video to one reviewer and they can forward it to another. The link keeps viewing easy without a Google account. The trade-off: your recipient list doesn't decide who can watch.
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177?hl=en

### 2/3

2/3 YouTube made unlisted videos uploaded before 2017 private in a 2021 security update unless owners opted out. Old links alone no longer granted access, and embeds stopped working. A tighter audience can break a distribution channel.

### 3/3

3/3 Use a shareable link for a help video you want customers to pass around. For an embargoed demo, default to named viewers. Before launch, test a forwarded link and an existing embed. Can the right viewer still watch after access changes?
