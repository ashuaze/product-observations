### 1/3

1/3 Buying a gift in Chrome Incognito can keep the visit out of local browsing history after all Incognito windows close. The shop may still observe the visit; your download stays on disk. A privacy mode needs a named observer and a clear end condition.

### 2/3

2/3 On a shared laptop, Incognito and Guest serve different jobs. Incognito keeps your profile's info and settings available. Guest hides other Chrome profiles' info within that session. Neither promises to hide activity from sites or network operators.

### 3/3

3/3 For a privacy feature, name what expires, what survives, and who can still observe. Then test those predictions. A 2018 browser study found explanations left important misconceptions intact; a mode label alone isn't evidence of understanding.
