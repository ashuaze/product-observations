### 1/4

1/4 Send a shift handoff with a 24-hour timer. Will it vanish before the next shift reads it, or will they have 24 hours with it once they do? The number on the timer can't tell you. You need to know when the clock starts.

### 2/4

2/4 In Signal, the sender's timer starts at send and the recipient's at read. WhatsApp counts from send, so an unread message can disappear from the chat. Kept messages are an exception.
https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360007320771-Set-and-manage-disappearing-messages
https://faq.whatsapp.com/673193694148537

### 3/4

3/4 A disappearing message won't revoke a door code. WhatsApp says a forward to a chat with timers off can survive. If a code must stop working at checkout, expire it in the lock system. Set the chat's timer separately.

### 4/4

4/4 Give late readers a reading window when the handoff still matters. Count from send when the original chat needs a predictable age limit. Test both with a late opener: did the note reach them, and what remained after the deadline?
