### 1/4

1/4 A geotagged photo can arrive without a location in a Google Photos share, then arrive with GPS attached when you email the downloaded file. Same picture, same recipient, different route. Test the copy the recipient gets.

### 2/4

2/4 Google Photos leaves location out of new shares by default, with an exception for Partner Sharing. Google says a download shared elsewhere can show the original device location, even if you edited the location in Photos.
https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6153599?hl=en

### 3/4

3/4 An archive or field team may need the original coordinates. For everyday sharing, I'd offer a copy without location. For handoffs that need the original, label that option with what it carries. The choice belongs where the file leaves the app.

### 4/4

4/4 A file without GPS can still show a street sign. Test the file's metadata and what's visible in the image separately. Have senders predict what a recipient will see in the album and in the attachment, then check both.
