### 1/4

1/4 A parcel is missing. Support could be waiting for your order number or for the carrier to trace the package. Both tickets are stuck. Only one needs you to do something. What should the status tell you?

### 2/4

2/4 Zendesk calls the first case Pending and the second On-hold, if that status is enabled. In the standard customer portal, Pending appears as "Awaiting your reply." On-hold appears as "Open."

### 3/4

3/4 The same wait can count differently, too. In Zendesk's SLA settings, Agent Work Time pauses in both states. Requester Wait Time pauses in Pending but keeps counting during On-hold. https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408829459866-Defining-SLA-policies

### 4/4

4/4 One Waiting label can work if you separately record who owes the next step and which clock keeps running. Test both parcel cases: does the customer know whether to reply, and does the carrier delay count toward the promise you made them?
