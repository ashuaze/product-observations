### 1/5

1/5 A private Pinterest board can still shape your home feed. That makes sense for a surprise party you’re planning. After the party, those saved ideas may be useful as memories while new party suggestions have lost their value.

### 2/5

2/5 Pinterest says only you and people you invite can see a private board. It may suggest Pins based on that board, visible only to you. Making a collection private and choosing whether it guides discovery answer different questions.

https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/secret-boards

### 3/5

3/5 In “Refine your recommendations,” the Activity tab lets you turn off recommendations based on Pins you’ve engaged with. Pinterest says these controls leave home-feed ads unchanged. The setting’s effect depends on the use of the signal.

https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/tune-your-home-feed

### 4/5

4/5 Automatically excluding private collections could sacrifice useful ideas during an active project. Keeping visibility and recommendation controls independent gives people more choice, with more to manage. For projects with an end, test completion as a recommendation signal.

### 5/5

5/5 When people mark a project finished, show which future uses will change. Keep their saved material. Test unwanted follow-ups and useful discoveries lost. Check what people expect to stop across recommendations and ads before treating one switch as enough.
