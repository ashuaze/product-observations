### 1/5

1/5 An iCloud device backup can be up to date without containing your photo library. With iCloud Photos on, synced photos are excluded from that backup. Turning on both services doesn't create two separate photo backups.

https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770

### 2/5

2/5 With iCloud Photos off, a device backup may include photos stored on the iPhone. With it on, photos sync separately. The inclusion list is conditional: the same backup setting can cover different data depending on another feature.

### 3/5

3/5 iCloud Photos syncs deletions across devices. Recently Deleted normally offers 30 days of recovery; Apple says deleted items bypass it if iCloud storage is over its limit. Sync has recovery features, but their conditions matter.

### 4/5

4/5 A computer backup of the iPhone also excludes data already synced and stored in iCloud, including iCloud Photos. Apple documents exporting photos to external storage separately; that copy needs its own update schedule.

https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306

### 5/5

5/5 For backup UX, show coverage, recovery location, and any deadline alongside the last successful run. If another feature changes coverage, surface that change. Test lost-device recovery and accidental deletion separately; one success doesn't establish the other.
