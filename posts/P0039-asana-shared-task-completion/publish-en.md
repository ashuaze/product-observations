### 1/4

1/4 A launch task sits on both the product and marketing boards. Someone checks it off. Are both teams done, or did one team just finish its part? The answer depends on whether those boards share a task or each own a deliverable.

### 2/4

2/4 In Asana, adding a task to several projects gives one task several homes. Complete it from one project and it's complete in all of them. That's useful when both teams need to track the same deliverable, like final screenshots. https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-fields

### 3/4

3/4 If product still owes the final screenshots and marketing still owes the launch email, give them separate tasks. Link them only where the order matters: marketing can draft early, but sending may need to wait for the final assets.

### 4/4

4/4 Before sharing a task across boards, ask each team what checking it off means. If both answers name the same deliverable, share it. If the answers differ, split the work and check whether the right person gets the signal to start.
